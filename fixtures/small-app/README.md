# small-app

Pricing rules for a small shop. All amounts are integer yen.

## discountAmount

Orders with a total of 1000 or more receive a discount of 100.

## shippingFee

Shipping is free for orders of 5000 or more. Otherwise shipping costs 500.

## loyaltyBonus

Members with points of 300 or more earn a bonus of 50.

## seniorPrice

Visitors aged 65 or older pay 800. Others pay 1200.

## bulkUnitPrice

Orders with a quantity of 100 or more cost 90 per unit.

## freeGiftCount

Orders with a total of 3000 or more include 1 free gift.

## expressFee

Express delivery for a distance of 50 or more costs 700.

## studentDiscount

Customers aged 18 or older get no student discount.

## reviewReward

Customers with reviews of 10 or more receive a reward of 20.

## vipLevel

Customers who spent 100000 or more reach level 2.
